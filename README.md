---

### 🔬 Research Interests
- **Logic for Specification and Verification** (particularly Many-Sorted Modal Logic)
- **Many-Valued Logics** (logical, algebraic, and probabilistic approaches)
- **Algebraic Logic, MV-algebras, Łukasiewicz Logic, and Non-Classical Logics**

---

### 🎓 Academic Leadership & Recognition
- **Former Dean**, Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Bucharest (Feb 2020 – Feb 2024)
- **"Grigore Moisil" Prize** of the Romanian Academy (2008, awarded in 2010)
- **Humboldt Fellow**, Technische Universität Darmstadt, Germany (2007–2009)
